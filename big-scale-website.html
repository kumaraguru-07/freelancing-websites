Common questions

How long does a website or app take?

Most simple shop websites take about a week from getting your details to going live. Apps and bigger projects take longer — we'll give you a clear timeline before starting.

What do you need from me to get started?

Your shop name, logo (if you have one), a few photos, your products or services, and contact details. If you don't have a logo yet, we can design one for you.

Do you handle hosting and the domain?

Yes — we can set up hosting and your domain name for you, or work with one you already own.

Can I ask for changes after it's built?

Yes. We include a round of revisions in every project, and we're reachable on WhatsApp after launch for anything that comes up.

How much does it cost?

It depends on what your shop needs — a single poster costs very different from a full website with an app. Message us on WhatsApp with what you're looking for and we'll give you a clear price.
